# First 90 seconds (long video; Shorts compress to 3 s hook + 20 s payoff)
0-5s    Greeting or cold line + what this video/episode is
5-20s   Why now: <real fact: date, result, what changed, why it matters to you>
20-35s  Who it is for + what is new/different
35-45s  One-line subscribe ask
45-70s  Setup (settings, gear, situation, rules of the challenge)
70-90s  The promise: "by the end you will have/see <X>" -> go
Then: pay off the title early, keep escalating, name each chapter aloud, quiet during action/demo, recap + next-episode line at the end.
