# Hook and retention (read before writing the hook cue and the beat map)

Distilled 2026-10-26 from creator-education sources (Subscribr, Opus, SocialKit, Pixflow and similar). These are rules of thumb, not YouTube policy. Their statistics (e.g. "80% at 3 s") are unverified: do not quote them. Check the user's own YouTube Studio retention graph after upload and feed what it shows back here.

## Hook (hook cue)
- Open on the result, the surprise, or the tension. Never greeting, channel name, logo, or "in this video".
- The hook must confirm the title/thumbnail promise to the right viewer. Overpromising gives a spike, then a drop.
- Shorts: payoff-first, first words by 0.35 s, one idea. Long: first words by 5 s; the style-card greeting may follow the hook only.
- Hook shapes (pick one, name it in the beat map): **result-first** (say the outcome, then rewind), **question with stakes**, **contrarian claim**, **teaser of the best moment**, **cold open + rewind**. Hinglish example of result-first: "Ek line ne Sony ka poora plan leak kar diya."

## Hold (setup and turn cues)
- Open a loop in the hook (a promise not yet paid). Close it only at the payoff cue. Do not close it early with a preview of the answer.
- Something new every ~30 s in long videos (a stake, a number card, a reversal, a `[your take]` reaction). Re-open a smaller loop as each one closes ("lekin asli problem ye nahi hai").
- Pattern interrupts: first around 25-35 s, then every 30-60 s. Make it verbal (a question, a tone drop, a short breather cue) or lean on the edit's own cut/zoom. Do not fake one with hype words.
- Listicles: tease the best item early; order from good to best, not best first.

## Payoff and end
- Shorts: end right after the payoff. No outro, no "like and subscribe". Optionally a 3-6 word callback to the hook so the loop feels deliberate and invites a rewatch. Only if the edit's last frame can carry it.
- Long: payoff, then one-line recommendation, then the style-card outro. A CTA exists only if the edit has a CTA card.
- Write hook and payoff first, then bridge. Cut the draft by about 30%.

## Check before render
1. Can the hook be said in one breath and still make sense without the picture?
2. Is there an open loop, and which cue closes it?
3. Longer than 4 min: a new stake in 2:30-3:30 (validator already warns).
4. Shorts: does the last cue land on the payoff, not trail off?
Record the hook shape and the loop's closing cue in `01-beat-map.md` under the first block.
