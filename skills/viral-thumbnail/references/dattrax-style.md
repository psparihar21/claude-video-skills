## What the numbers say
- Views 0.7M to 3.3M per video. Top: Palworld EP1 (3.3M), GTA 5 Grand RP with a bigger creator (2.9M, 2.6M, 2.0M, 1.6M), Black Myth Wukong #01 (2.7M), Korean-war COD #1 (1.6M), TLOU Part 1 (1.5M), God of War #1 (1.4M).

- Collabs with a large creator in a shared GTA RP server = second-biggest cluster.

- Upload cadence unknown (About page not readable without login).

## Title formula (all caps, English with Hindi-audience cadence)
1. `NEW <THING> BEGINS!` / `A NEW JOURNEY BEGINS` / `WELCOME TO <PLACE>!` + `[Game] #01` - the ep-1 template. Most-used.
2. `FINALLY <MILESTONE>!` - `FINALLY MY FIRST LEGENDARY POKEMON`, `FINALLY BOUGHT MY FIRST SUPERBIKE`, `FINALLY I'M THE MONKEY KING`.
3. `<HERO> VS <BOSS>!` for boss episodes - `WUKONG VS THE WHITE DRAGON`.
4. Stakes + number: `WE SCAMMED $20,000,000 FROM BANK`, `SPENDING $50,000 IN MY SUPERMARKET`.
5. Collab: `I JOINED <CREATOR> IN GTA 5 GRAND RP`, `ATTACK ON <CREATOR> GANG`. Creator handle in the title.
Format: exclamation at end, game name in brackets or after a pipe, `#NN` episode tag. 55-80 chars. No Hindi script in the title.

## Thumbnail formula (every one of the 14 follows it)
- 1280x720, **face fixed on the right 30-35 percent**, shoulder-up, same position and scale every time. Wide-eyed open-mouth shock or a thumbs-up smile. Red or yellow t-shirt = one saturated block at bottom-right.
- Left 65 percent = the game's hero scene: key art or a cinematic frame, very high saturation and contrast (boosted), a single focal subject (character from behind, boss, explosion, bike).
- Text: 0-3 words only, and often **no title text at all** (Wukong, TLOU rely on the game logo). When used: chunky white or yellow caps on a colored rounded plate (green, red) with dark outline: `FIRST DAY IN NEW CITY`, `JAIL ESCAPE`, `20$ MILLION SCAM`, `FINALLY BOUGHT NEW BIKE!`.
- Episode: white `#1` / `#15` bottom-left in bold with dark stroke.
- Collab videos: collaborators' real faces on the left, bright text plate above.
- One red arrow or glow on the key detail (Palworld pal) when the frame is busy.
- Same layout across hundreds of videos = brand recognition. Consistency is the style.

## Observed: Wukong #01 (52:47, 2.7M views, 1.04M subs) - first 16 min of the Hindi auto transcript + one frame
Only this one video is studied in depth. Get a transcript by opening the "Show transcript" panel on a watch page (the direct caption URL returns empty).
- **Hook (0-30s)**: greeting ("what's up gamers, kaise ho") -> news-speed claim ("game released at 7 this morning and the video is already here") -> what the game is + who it's for ("if you like action-adventure you'll enjoy this series") -> hype claim ("best realistic graphics of any game") -> self-deprecating joke ("my PC will probably die") -> "let's go". No cold open, no clip teaser. The first frame is the host's face cam with a burned-in Hindi caption (screenshot at 0:03).
- **Setup (30-70s)**: shows his graphics settings ("everything maxed, today we make the system cry"), then promises the whole thing: "I'll tell the complete story and show every cutscene". That promise is the retention hook for a 50-min video.
- **Delivery**: unscripted live reaction in Hinglish: "bhai", "yaar", "OMG", "look at the graphics", questions to the chat ("what is this panther?"), then explains the lore in his own words (Monkey King story, 3:14-5:00, 7:12, 11:23). Mixes explanation of the cutscene with reaction. Game cutscene audio plays underneath (English lines in the transcript); he talks over gaps.
- **Cadence**: a spoken segment about every 8-10 s early, then long stretches of game audio/music during fights (6:34-10:50 mostly `[संगीत]`). Roughly 130-170 spoken words a minute while he talks, near-silent during action.
- **Structure**: intro 1 min -> prologue cutscene with story retelling to ~15:00 -> "Chapter 1" title said aloud at 15:10 ("now the real story starts"), episode continues into gameplay.
- **Description (that video)**: SEO line first ("Black Myth Wukong Gameplay Walkthrough PC With Commentary 2160p 60fps HD let's play playthrough review guide"), then "Showcasing all cutscenes movie edition, all boss fights, side missions, upgrades, outfits, all characters, best moments, final boss and true ending". 4K 60 fps is a selling point.
- **Not yet checked**: his burned-in caption style (font/color), zoom or facecam layout during gameplay, SFX, music beds, outro. Needs frame sampling of a playable (not age-gated) video.

### What repeats in all three videos
1. Greeting + "new journey/series/episode" in the first 5 s.
2. Why this matters now (release-day speed, childhood love, the collab).
3. One concrete promise of what will happen.
4. A call to action inside the first 90 s (subscribe, or sponsor link in description).
5. "Let's go" then into play, jokes about NPCs, then spoken story retelling.
6. Hinglish with English game terms; filler words "bhai", "yaar", "guys".

## Script and video recipe (inferred from titles/durations only)
- Ep 1: "new journey begins" framing, game intro and first impressions, play start to first set-piece; 50-90 min.
- Milestone episodes carry the title promise within the first 10 min, then keep escalating.
- Series numbering in every title; boss fights get their own episode.
- Reaction-first commentary is the channel's brand (inferred from the shock face); our voice rules in faceless-vo-script / own-voice-render still apply.
- Hook must pay off the title: if the title says "FINALLY BOUGHT BIKE", show the bike inside the first minutes.
