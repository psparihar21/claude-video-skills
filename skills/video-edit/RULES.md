# video-edit RULES

RULES.md overrides SKILL.md. Read this file at the start of every run.
Add new rules at the bottom, one per correction, dated, in the user's words. Remove a rule only when the user says to remove that rule.

## Starter rules (2026-10-14)

- All 7 tools are required. Name any skip.
- SnipSound is the only music and effect source.
- Parakeet timestamps drive cuts and captions.
- Wait for "yes" on edit-decision.md before the final render.
- Tech long form is 16:9. Shorts are 9:16.
- Gameplay HUD stays fully visible during action.
- Captions stay clear of the facecam.
- Keep a license line for every audio file.
- Do not invent product facts. Chrome sources only.

## My style (chosen by the user, 2026-10-14)

- Language: "Hindi or Hinglish". Hindi/Hinglish speech is transcribed with GPU Whisper large-v3; English-only speech uses Parakeet. Whichever engine ran, its word timestamps drive cuts and captions.
- Pacing: "Balanced". Remove dead air, ums and retakes; keep natural breathing pauses.
- Captions: "Clean minimal". Small clean line at the bottom, no animation, no word highlight.
- Caption letters: "English letters ("yeh video dekho")". Hindi/Hinglish captions and SRT are in Roman Hinglish, never Devanagari. English loanwords use normal English spelling (video, settings, click).
- DaVinci Resolve: "Only when really needed". Otherwise FFmpeg does the edit.
